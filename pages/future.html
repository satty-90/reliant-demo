Where We're Investing

Three Areas Shaping the Next Decade

Each of these is a direction we're moving in, at a pace that matches real customer demand — not a promise of what already exists today.

Concept
01 — Automation

Robotics & Automation

Expanding automation across material handling and inspection — reducing cycle variation and freeing up skilled operators for higher-value work.

Concept
02 — EV Programs

Electric Vehicle Components

Positioning our stamping, welding, and assembly capability for EV-specific parts — battery enclosures, structural brackets, and body components built to new tolerances.

Extending our press capability toward larger structural body panels — built on the same press and tooling discipline behind everything we run today.
