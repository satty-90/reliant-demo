Our Journey

The Reliant timeframe.

Key moments in the Reliant story, from the first day to today.

  1. Chapter 01 1999

    The First Frame

    Every journey starts with a belief.

    Reliant Industries Inc. is established by Don Zack, building on two decades of tool and die experience.

  2. Chapter 02 2000

    The Work Begins

    From an idea to real operations.

    Reliant begins operating as an off-load stamper and factory assembly support supplier.

  3. Chapter 03 The Growth Years

    Expanding the Picture

    A broader role in manufacturing.

    The business develops into two divisions: first-operation blanks, and full-service stampings and assemblies for production and service requirements.

  4. Chapter 04 2026

    Built on Standards

    A commitment to consistent quality.

    Quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 by NSF-ISR. The current certificate was issued in May 2026.

  5. Chapter 05 Today

    The Bigger Picture

    The company Reliant has become.

    A 50,000+ square-foot facility in Sterling Heights, Michigan.
