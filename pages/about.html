Chapter 01 — The Beginning

Before the facility, there was a vision.

After two decades in the tool and die industry, Don Zack set out to build Reliant Industries. What began with experience and a belief in doing the job right would become the foundation of the company’s story.

“I believe manufacturing is about more than producing parts. It is about identifying problems, understanding our customers’ challenges, and finding practical solutions that create measurable value. This is the type of partnership we strive to build with every customer.”

Don ZackFounder
Our Journey

The Reliant timeframe.

Key moments in the Reliant story, from the first day to today.

  1. Chapter 01 1980

    Built on Tool & Die

    Where it all began.

    Tool and die building is the foundation of Reliant and part of its DNA.

  2. Chapter 02 1999

    Reliant Is Born

    Every journey starts with a belief.

    Reliant Industries Inc. is established by Don Zack, building on two decades of tool and die experience. Early on, the company works as an off-load stamper and factory assembly support supplier.

  3. Chapter 03 2010

    Room to Grow

    A new location and more capacity.

    Reliant relocates and expands its operations.

  4. Chapter 04 2024

    Revenue Triples

    A breakthrough year.

    Reliant’s revenue triples.

  5. Chapter 05 2026

    Built on Standards

    A commitment to consistent quality.

    Quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 by NSF-ISR. The current certificate was issued in May 2026.

  6. Chapter 06 Today

    The Bigger Picture

    The company Reliant has become.

    A 50,000+ square-foot facility in Sterling Heights, Michigan, with two divisions: first-operation blanks, and full-service stampings and assemblies for production and service requirements.
