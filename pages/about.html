Who We Are

More Than a Stamping Supplier.

Reliant Industries was built around a simple idea: manufacturing customers need more than a supplier that can run a press. They need a partner who understands the component, the tooling, what the part actually needs on the floor, and the realities of getting it into repeatable production.

Don Zack started Reliant Industries after 20 years in the tool and die industry. That foundation continues to shape the company today, with manufacturing experience spanning tooling, stamping, blanking, welding, and assembly.

As an American, family-owned manufacturer, Reliant provides the responsiveness and flexibility customers need when specifications shift, additional capacity is required, or a component demands more than a single manufacturing process.
