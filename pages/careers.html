Press Operator

Production · Reports to Manufacturing / Production Manager

Job summary

Configures and operates machine press equipment to shape materials used in manufacturing operations. Performs operations such as blanking, bending, punching and forming on a variety of materials and equipment, based on specific documented instructions. Works under general supervision and is expected to maintain excellent attendance.

Essential job functions

  1. Basic machine operations, including cycling and setting counting devices, recognizing the condition of dies, loading material onto the equipment, feeding material through the die, and positioning and removing parts from the die.
  2. Operate the crane for material loading.
  3. Keep a record of the number of pieces produced, along with other necessary documentation.
  4. Inspect parts on an established schedule to make sure produced parts meet specifications.
  5. Read the work schedule to find production information such as size, type and quantity to be processed.
  6. Other duties as assigned.

Minimum requirements

High school diploma or equivalent with 1–3 years of experience in the field or a related area. Familiar with standard concepts, practices and procedures in a stamping environment. Relies on limited experience and judgment to plan and accomplish goals. Must be able to carry out the physical requirements of the role, which involves physical movement.

Material Handler / Hi-Lo Driver

Production / Shipping · Reports to Manufacturing Manager and Materials Manager

Job summary

Responsible for the safe operation of company equipment when storing, loading, unloading or moving material within or near the plant, yard or worksite.

Essential job functions

  1. Inspect the Hi-Lo at the start of each shift before operating it, and report any inoperable functions to the supervisor.
  2. Confirm and unload incoming material against the invoice and place it in the designated receiving area.
  3. Load outgoing material as specified by the Materials Manager.
  4. Secure the lifting attachment to material when using a hand-powered crane or hoist.
  5. Remove and properly dispose of all engineering scrap from the production floor.
  6. Maintain and follow FIFO practices.
  7. Make sure all production jobs are properly stocked with product and the applicable packaging.
  8. Make sure all material stored in WIP is clearly identified with part number and quantity.
  9. Before shipping, verify part number and quantity with the shipper.
  10. At the end of the workday, return the Hi-Lo to its designated area and, if applicable, plug it into the charger.
  11. Some packaging or transferring of material may be required.
  12. May assist the die setter in removing or setting dies.
  13. Other duties as assigned.

Minimum requirements

High school diploma or equivalent with 3–5 years of experience in the field or a related area. Familiar with standard concepts, practices and procedures in a stamping environment.

Die Setter

Production · Reports to Tool Room Manager

Job summary

Responsible for the setup or changeover of line and progressive dies in an efficient and timely manner (60 minutes or less).

Essential job functions

  1. Have the press ready at the start of the day and shut it down at the end of the day.
  2. Complete die setups and teardowns in 60 minutes or less.
  3. Follow the first- and last-piece approval process.
  4. Pre-stage everything needed for the next setup, using the press assignment schedule supplied by the Tool Room or Production Manager.
  5. Train operators, sharing knowledge so they can take part in the die change process.
  6. Troubleshoot and correct issues that hinder efficiency or create non-conformances or an unsafe work environment.
  7. Make sure the right containers, packing materials and scrap hoppers are in place (details are on the Process Sheet, supplied by the Tool Room Manager).
  8. Assist operators with coil changes and the start of strip.
  9. Make sure all safety equipment is operational and that the setup is ergonomically sound.
  10. Create and update die setup sheets and keep them accurate.
  11. Communicate between shifts.
  12. Other duties as assigned.
  13. Must have your own tools.
  14. Must have line and progressive tooling experience.

Minimum requirements

High school diploma or equivalent with 3–5 years of experience in the field or a related area. Familiar with standard concepts, practices and procedures in a stamping environment.

Die Repairman

Tool Room · Reports to Tool Room Manager

Job summary

Responsible for maintaining die tooling and building new tools. Works closely with manufacturing to make sure tools are operational and functional, supporting the quality requirements and the production schedule.

Essential job functions

  1. Operate all tool room machinery and equipment.
  2. Read blueprints.
  3. Adjust tooling to produce parts that fit check fixtures.
  4. Follow verbal instructions as well as written work instructions.
  5. Know safety lock-out procedures.
  6. Have the tools of the trade.
  7. Other duties as assigned.

Minimum requirements

Minimum of 5 years of experience in the tool room or die build environment. Weld experience (MIG and TIG) is a plus.

Shipping / Receiving

Shipping · Reports to Materials Manager

Job summary

Responsible for all incoming and outgoing shipments to the warehouse.

Essential job functions

  1. Know how to operate a Hi-Lo and an overhead crane, and how to use the scale.
  2. Handle all incoming shipments: unloading, and verifying paperwork for correct quantities, items, weight and so on.
  3. Handle all outgoing shipments, making sure the correct part and quantity go out. All outgoing parts are weighed before shipment, any discrepancies are reported immediately, and a signed shipper is required.
  4. Unload and confirm incoming steel, and make sure paperwork is turned in so tags can be issued in a timely manner.
  5. Back up the production Hi-Lo drivers as needed.
  6. Keep the warehouse clean and organized, including finished goods, WIP and steel. All containers and coils must be tagged.
  7. Other duties as assigned.

Minimum requirements

High school diploma or equivalent with 3–5 years of experience in the field or a related area. Familiar with standard concepts, practices and procedures in a stamping environment.
