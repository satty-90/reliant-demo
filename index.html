Our Manufacturing Process

From raw metal coil and tooling engineering to precision stamping, blanking, and welded assembly — built for repeatability at scale.

01 — Stamping

Precision Metal Stamping

30 to 800-ton press capacity engineered for complex geometric forming and strict automotive tolerances.

02 — Blanking

Blanking

Cut-to-length and configured shapes from coils up to 60,000 lb and material widths up to 72 inches.

03 — Tooling

Tooling & Die Engineering

In-house tool and die design, rapid maintenance, custom fixtures, and precise die adjustments.

04 — Assembly

Welding & Secondary Assembly

Manual or automated welding and assembly of stamped, welded, or coated material into completed assemblies.

Our Story

Our Journey & Milestones

A family-owned Michigan manufacturer, built on a tool and die background and grown into a full-service stamping and assembly supplier.

Established
1999

Reliant Industries Inc. is established by Don Zack, who started the company after 20 years in the tool and die industry.

Operations
2000

Begins operating as an off-load stamper and factory assembly support supplier.
